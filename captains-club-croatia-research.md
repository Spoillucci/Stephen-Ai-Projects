# Captains Club research: Croatia

**Dates:** May 16–19, 2027 (3 nights)
**Group:** 16 rooms / approximately 32 adults
**Budget:** USD $2,500 all-in per room for the stay
**Gates applied (adults-only dropped):** officially marketed 5-star; true all-inclusive (meals **and** beverages, including premium spirits and wines, in the rate — not optional packages, meal credits, or limited-hour plans); 3+ à la carte restaurants (buffets do not count); luxury comparable to Secrets / Impression / Sandals Royal Barbados / Excellence / Valentin Imperial / Maxx Royal; ≤40 minutes by road from an international airport; dedicated groups/events capability; modern suites; no boat/ferry/seaplane/helicopter arrival.

**Headline:** Croatia produced one property that clears most gates — **Rixos Premium Dubrovnik** — but the true all-inclusive gate is uncertain and must be verified by RFP. No other Croatia property comes close. Croatia's all-inclusive market is dominated by family waterpark resorts (Valamar) and budget operators (Pine Beach); the luxury tier is almost entirely B&B or half-board.

### The true all-inclusive question — the controlling issue

This is the single gate that determines whether Rixos Premium Dubrovnik qualifies, and the evidence is conflicting:

**Evidence that it IS a true AI:**
- The Rixos brand is fundamentally an all-inclusive chain (Rixos Premium Belek, Sharm El Sheikh, etc. are all AI resorts).
- The Rixos website has a dedicated "What is included in an All Inclusive stay" section listing buffet/à la carte dining, sports club, pools, hammam, sauna, entertainment.
- One third-party luxury review describes a "Rixos premium all-inclusive" package that "includes every restaurant à la carte, a deep wine and spirit programme, the in-room minibar, the spa circuit, and the daily activity programme" and states rates "include the all-inclusive luxury programme."

**Evidence that it is NOT a true AI (stronger weight):**
- The **official 2025 factsheet** lists the concept as **"Bed & Breakfast"** — not all-inclusive.
- The **Accor official hotel page** describes B&B and half-board concepts, buffet breakfast, and bars — with no comprehensive AI package described.
- **Resort Flock** (review aggregator) explicitly warns: "The resort primarily operates on B&B/Half Board; guests expecting a full all-inclusive package with all meals and drinks should verify inclusions carefully as many items cost extra." It lists à la carte restaurants (Umi Teppanyaki, Libertas Fish, Mykorini) as **"Available at Extra Cost"** and "All alcoholic and most non-alcoholic beverages" as extra cost.
- The third-party review's restaurant list (L'Olivo, Asian Corner, Steakhouse, Anatolian Heritage) does not match the official factsheet's restaurant list (Turquoise, Umi Teppanyaki, Libertas Fish, Mykorini), undermining that source's reliability for this property.

**Assessment:** The weight of official evidence indicates that Rixos Premium Dubrovnik is a **luxury hotel that primarily sells B&B and half-board**, with an optional premium all-inclusive package available — not a true all-inclusive resort where AI is the product. This is closer to the Shangri-La Mactan or Cape Sounio profile than to Ikos or Riu Palace. The third-party review may describe a real AI package that exists but is not the default board basis, or may be inaccurate.

**RFP must confirm in writing:**
1. Is a comprehensive all-inclusive package available for May 16–19, 2027?
2. Does it include all 3 à la carte restaurants with unlimited reservations?
3. Does it include premium spirits and premium wines (not just house wine/beer)?
4. Does it include the in-room minibar with alcoholic beverages?
5. Is it truly unlimited, or are there caps/rationing?
6. What is the per-room rate for the AI package for the 3-night stay?

### Fit summary

Rixos Premium Dubrovnik is the **only Croatia property** that could plausibly pass the Captains Club gates. It clears 5-star, transfer (20–30 min from DBV), luxury, groups/events, modern suites, and no-boat-transfer cleanly. It has exactly 3 a la carte restaurants (conditional on seasonal opening in mid-May). The **controlling issue is true all-inclusive**: official sources indicate the hotel operates primarily on B&B/half-board, with an optional AI package. If the RFP confirms a comprehensive, unlimited AI package (all restaurants, premium spirits and wines, minibar) at a rate within budget, this becomes a clean qualifier. If the AI is limited, rationed, or unavailable for the dates, it fails the true-AI gate.

**Advantages over Greece options:**
- Shortest international transfer of any contender (~20–30 min from DBV)
- UNESCO Old Town walkable from the hotel — unmatched cultural/excursion density
- Rixos luxury brand directly comparable to Maxx Royal
- Strong events infrastructure (7 meeting rooms, 900-guest capacity)

**Disadvantages vs. Greece options:**
- True all-inclusive is uncertain (Ikos Infinite Lifestyle is confirmed true AI)
- Only 3 a la carte (vs. 7 at Ikos) and 2 of those are seasonal
- Family hotel with kids' club (Ikos Deluxe Collection offers better executive isolation)
- Not a sandy beach — wooden sea deck with ladders (vs. beachfront at Ikos/Mitsis)
- Room sizes smaller (Superior Sea View 30 m² vs. Ikos Deluxe suites)
