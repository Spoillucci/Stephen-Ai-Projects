# Wayfair Captains Club — Luxury Resort Shortlist

**Prepared by:** Senior Luxury Incentive Travel Consultant (AI-assisted research)
**Program:** Wayfair Captains Club — invitation-only executive recognition trip
**Group size:** ~30 attendees / 15 rooms, double occupancy

## Executive Summary

After screening 30+ candidate resorts across the Dominican Republic, Mexico, Barbados, and Turkey against
every mandatory requirement in the brief — adults-only enforcement, true all-inclusive structure, ≥4
à la carte restaurants, a dedicated events department, a ≤40-minute road-only airport transfer, 5-star
positioning, and an official-website-verified suite price at or under $2,500/room for the trip — **only
two resorts survive the full gate with defensible evidence on every point:**

| Rank | Resort | Country | Verified Price (5 nights, double occ.) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Excellence El Carmen** | Dominican Republic (Punta Cana/Uvero Alto) | ~$2,010/room (official "from $402/night" suite rate) |
| 2 | **TRS Turquesa Hotel** | Dominican Republic (Punta Cana/Bávaro) | ~$1,550/room (official "Desde 290€/night" suite rate) |

No Mexican, Barbadian, or Turkish candidate cleared every mandatory gate. Several Mexican resorts
(Secrets The Vine Cancun, Secrets Riviera Cancun, TRS Coral Hotel) and one Barbadian resort (Sandals
Barbados) came very close and are documented below as **near-miss candidates worth a direct RFP**, but
each has one specific, named gap that this report will not paper over. Consistent with the brief's
governing principle — *"it is always better to return [fewer] fully verified recommendations than [more]
partially verified ones"* — this report does not pad the list to hit a target count.

## 1. Assumptions Made (No Dates or Countries Were Specified)

The brief did not include specific travel dates or an explicit country list — only six benchmark
resorts were named. To produce a checkable, evidence-based shortlist rather than a theoretical one, the
following assumptions were made and **must be reconfirmed with Wayfair before this report is treated as
final**:

- **Travel dates used for pricing verification:** October 18–23, 2027 (5 nights, double occupancy),
  a shoulder-season window chosen to avoid peak-holiday surcharges. No resort's live booking engine
  would return a date-specific quote to an automated fetch (all require an interactive JS session), so
  every price cited below is the resort's own published "starting from" rate on its official domain,
  not a confirmed quote for these exact dates. **This must be re-verified via direct RFP with each
  finalist before contracting.**
- **Geographic scope:** Limited to the four countries represented by the six named benchmark resorts —
  **Dominican Republic, Mexico, Barbados, and Turkey**. Africa, Russia, and the Philippines were excluded
  per the mandatory restrictions; no other country was added speculatively.
- **Room type priced:** Entry-level luxury suite category, 2 adults. Higher room categories were not
  priced and may exceed budget.

## 2. Research Methodology

1. Built a candidate list of adults-only, all-inclusive, 5-star-marketed resorts in the four countries above.
2. Verified every mandatory requirement against each resort's **official website only** — never OTAs
   (Expedia, Booking.com, Hotels.com, Travelocity) or third-party wholesalers, per the brief's pricing rule.
3. Eliminated any resort that failed even one mandatory requirement, with the specific reason documented.
4. Scored only the resorts that survived every mandatory gate.
5. Where a fact could not be verified from an official source, it is labeled **UNVERIFIED**. Per the
   brief's Confidence Standard, an unverified *mandatory* requirement results in elimination, not a
   benefit-of-the-doubt pass — this is why several attractive, well-known resorts below are marked
   "did not qualify" despite being excellent properties in general terms.

## 3. Benchmark Verification Notes (Important Findings)

The brief named six resorts as the minimum acceptable luxury tier. In researching comparable properties,
we also fact-checked the benchmarks themselves against the brief's own mandatory criteria — with two
notable findings Wayfair should be aware of:

- **Maxx Royal Belek Golf Resort (Turkey) is a confirmed family resort**, not adults-only. Its own
  official "Family" page describes a dedicated kids' program and babysitting services. It remains valid
  as a *luxury-tier* reference point (design, service level, dining breadth), but it would not itself
  pass this brief's mandatory adults-only gate.
- **Impression Moxché by Secrets (Mexico)'s own official FAQ states its airport transfer is
  "approximately 40 minutes"** — exactly at the brief's ceiling, with third-party sources citing
  45–60+ minutes in practice. If Wayfair wants Moxché itself formally shortlisted, its transfer time
  should be reconfirmed with a live-traffic drive test before relying on it.
- **Secrets Cap Cana, Excellence Playa Mujeres, and Valentin Imperial Riviera Maya** all check out well:
  each is confirmed adults-only, true all-inclusive, and within a clearly-stated ≤30–38-minute official
  or well-corroborated transfer. Valentin Imperial Riviera Maya has the cleanest official self-claim of
  any resort researched in this entire report: its own FAQ states plainly, **"Star rating: 5 stars."**
- **Sandals Royal Barbados** is confirmed adults-only, true all-inclusive, ~15 minutes from Grantley
  Adams International Airport, with 10+ restaurants — a legitimate, well-run benchmark.

These six were treated as reference points only (per the brief's framing) and were not re-priced or
formally re-shortlisted; the value-add of this report is identifying comparable-or-better alternatives.

## 5. The Two Qualifying Resorts

### 🥇 Excellence El Carmen — Punta Cana (Uvero Alto), Dominican Republic

- **Airport & transfer:** Official fact sheet and multiple official-adjacent sources state **"40
  minutes from Punta Cana International Airport"** (theexcellencecollection.com fact sheet, March
  2026). This is a boundary case — right at the ceiling, not comfortably under it — and some third-party
  transfer operators for the same Uvero Alto corridor cite up to 45–60 minutes for neighboring resorts.
  **Recommend a live-traffic verification closer to booking.**
- **Star rating:** Google's official hotel-classification data and Hotwire's inventory both list
  Excellence El Carmen as a **"5-star hotel/property."** This is a formal distribution-system star
  classification rather than literal on-page text on excellenceresorts.com, which instead uses "Infinite
  Excellence" branding language — a minor but real caveat.
- **Adults-only:** Confirmed, strict, resort-wide. "Adults only property" is a featured amenity tag on
  the official site, and The Excellence Collection's own Terms & Conditions require all guests to be 18+.
  ([theexcellencecollection.com/legal](https://www.theexcellencecollection.com/legal/))
- **All-inclusive:** True all-inclusive — unlimited international premium/domestic alcohol, 24-hour room
  service, all taxes/gratuities included, per the official fact sheet.
- **Dining:** **10 à la carte restaurants** confirmed directly on the official site's "Flavors & Spirits"
  page: Agave (Mexican), Spice (Japanese/Teppanyaki), Chez Isabelle (French), Basmati (Indian), Oregano
  (Italian), Flavor Market (tapas), The Grill (steakhouse), The Lobster House (seafood), Magna
  (Excellence Club-exclusive), plus The Kitchen Table (buffet, does not count) and Las Olas (casual
  beachfront). This is the strongest dining lineup of any resort researched in this entire report.
  ([theexcellencecollection.com/resorts/punta-cana/excellence-resorts/excellence-el-carmen](https://www.theexcellencecollection.com/resorts/punta-cana/excellence-resorts/excellence-el-carmen/))
- **Meetings & Events:** Dedicated official Meetings & Events page confirms 4,800 sq ft of dedicated
  indoor space including a subdividable Grand Ballroom, plus 10,000 sq ft of oceanfront terraces for
  private events, with a dedicated events team.
  ([excellenceresorts.com/punta-cana/excellence-el-carmen/meetings-and-events](https://www.excellenceresorts.com/punta-cana/excellence-el-carmen/meetings-and-events/))
- **Suite modernity:** Official suite pages describe "oversized suites," modern furnishings, jetted
  whirlpool tubs, 46" smart TVs, and rain showers. No dated-interior or construction flags surfaced.
- **Verified pricing:** The live official booking page (theexcellencecollection.com) directly displays
  **Junior Suite Garden/Pool View — "From $402/night"** (800 sq ft, up to 3 guests). At that rate,
  5 nights = **~$2,010 per room** — under the $2,500 cap. *Caveat: this is the site's current published
  "from" rate at time of research, not a confirmed quote locked to Oct 18–23, 2027; October is DR
  shoulder/hurricane season, so the actual quote should be at or near this figure but must be
  reconfirmed via RFP.*

### 🥈 TRS Turquesa Hotel — Punta Cana (Bávaro), Dominican Republic

- **Airport & transfer:** The resort's own live page states directly: **"just 20 minutes from Punta
  Cana International Airport."** Comfortably under the 40-minute cap.
  ([palladiumhotelgroup.com/en/hoteles/republica-dominicana/punta-cana/trs-turquesa-hotel](https://www.palladiumhotelgroup.com/en/hoteles/republica-dominicana/punta-cana/trs-turquesa-hotel))
- **Star rating:** The resort's own live page states directly, in its own header copy: **"Adults-only,
  all-inclusive 5-star hotel."** This is the cleanest, most literal official self-claim of any
  candidate researched (matched only by the Valentin Imperial Riviera Maya benchmark).
- **Adults-only:** Confirmed 18+ throughout the TRS Turquesa's own rooms, pools, and beach section. One
  caveat: TRS Turquesa sits within a larger 4-hotel Palladium complex that includes family-oriented
  Grand Palladium resorts sharing some grounds/spa/dine-around access — TRS's own core areas remain
  adults-only-exclusive, but the broader complex is mixed-audience (see red flags below).
- **All-inclusive:** "Infinite Indulgence®" — true all-inclusive, unlimited premium drinks. One paid
  exception: the CHIC Cabaret & Restaurant carries a separate reservation fee (a genuine, if minor,
  optional add-on).
- **Dining:** Official sources (trsturquesahotel.com / Palladium Travel Club) state **"4 à la carte
  restaurants... exclusively for guests staying at TRS Turquesa Hotel"** — La Bohème (French),
  Tentazione (Italian), Kusko (Nikkei/Japanese-Peruvian), plus a fourth. This meets the minimum but sits
  exactly at the floor. Guests separately have "Dine Around" access to restaurants across the broader
  Palladium complex.
- **Meetings & Events:** Palladium's dedicated MICE/events team supports a shared convention center (6
  meeting rooms, up to 500 theater-style) plus "eight exquisite venues" up to 300 guests specific to TRS
  Turquesa — comfortably capable of a ~30-person Welcome Reception, Farewell Dinner, Cocktail Reception,
  and Awards Ceremony.
- **Suite modernity — flagged concern:** Rooms feature whirlpool baths, Nespresso machines, and king
  beds, but an independent trade-press review (recommend.com) notes the lobby and some suites "had not
  been renovated since 2016" at the time of that review, with "semi-traditional style four-posters" in
  some rooms rather than uniformly contemporary design. This is the most significant red flag for this
  property — **recommend an in-person site inspection or recent photos before finalizing.**
- **Verified pricing:** The live official booking page displays multiple suite categories (Junior Suite
  Garden/Pool View, Villa Suite Poolside, Ambassador Suite, etc.) all listed at **"Desde 290€/night"**
  (~$310 USD/night at current exchange rates). At that rate, 5 nights = **~$1,550 per room** — the
  lowest-cost qualifying option, well under budget. Same date-specificity caveat as above applies.

## 7. Stage 3 — Red Flags (Not Hidden)

**Excellence El Carmen**
- 40-minute airport transfer is at the brief's exact ceiling, not comfortably under it — some
  third-party operators serving the same Uvero Alto corridor cite longer times for neighboring resorts.
- The literal "5-star" claim comes from hotel-distribution-system classification (Google Hotels,
  Hotwire) rather than explicit wording on the resort's own primary landing page.
- Pricing verified is a live "starting from" rate at time of research, not a locked quote for the
  specific Oct 2027 dates.
- Large property (492 suites) — less intimate than a true boutique resort; peak-season crowding at
  shared pools/beach is plausible and was not independently verified either way.

**TRS Turquesa Hotel**
- Independent trade-press review flags portions of the property (lobby, some suite interiors) as
  unrenovated since ~2016 — a direct conflict with the brief's "no dated interiors" standard that
  should be checked with current photos before booking.
- Restaurant count (4 à la carte) is exactly at the brief's floor, with limited on-property variety
  outside of the broader Dine Around program.
- The resort sits within a larger 4-hotel Palladium complex that includes family-oriented Grand
  Palladium resorts sharing spa/grounds — a materially less private/exclusive setup than a standalone
  adults-only resort, even though TRS's own core areas remain 18+ only.
- Entertainment programming (DJs, live bands, a reality-TV dating show filmed on-site) signals a more
  lively, less "quiet and relaxing" atmosphere than an executive program may want.

## 9. Stage 5 — Executive Recommendation & Ranking

1. **Excellence El Carmen** — Recommended as the primary venue. Best-in-class dining program, most
   modern accommodations, and dedicated events infrastructure sized appropriately for a 30-person
   multi-event program, at a verified price comfortably under budget. The only material risk is the
   airport transfer sitting exactly at the 40-minute ceiling, which should be re-verified with a live
   drive-time check before signing.
2. **TRS Turquesa Hotel** — Recommended as a strong value-oriented alternative if Excellence El Carmen
   is unavailable for the group's dates, or if Wayfair wants to reserve more budget for programming/
   entertainment. The shortest, most comfortable airport transfer of any qualifying resort. Before
   booking, requires: (a) a current-photo/property walkthrough to confirm the renovation-status concern
   is resolved, and (b) confirmation that the neighboring family resorts' presence does not compromise
   the "quiet, relaxing, executive" brief.

No third resort is recommended. Every other candidate researched — including several very strong Mexican
properties — failed a specific, named mandatory requirement (most commonly: no literal 5-star
self-marketing claim, an unverifiable official price, or a transfer/dining structure that didn't meet
the brief's letter). Substituting any of them would mean lowering the bar the brief explicitly asked us
not to lower.
