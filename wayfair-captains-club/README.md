# Wayfair Captains Club — Luxury Resort Shortlist

**Prepared by:** Senior Luxury Incentive Travel Consultant (AI-assisted research)
**Program:** Wayfair Captains Club — invitation-only executive recognition trip
**Group size:** ~30 attendees / 15 rooms, double occupancy
**Status:** DRAFT — pending final verification of pricing/availability against Wayfair's confirmed travel dates

---

## 1. Purpose

This document identifies, evaluates, and ranks luxury all-inclusive resorts capable of hosting Wayfair's
Captains Club — a Fortune 500 executive recognition program, not a family vacation. Every candidate was
run through a mandatory qualification gate before any scoring or ranking occurred. Resorts that failed
even one mandatory requirement were eliminated and are **not** included in the final recommendations,
regardless of how well they scored on other dimensions.

Quality over quantity governs this report. If only a handful of resorts survive qualification, only
that handful is recommended.

## 2. Assumptions Made (No Dates or Countries Were Specified)

The brief did not include:

- **Specific travel dates** for the trip.
- **Specific countries** the client wants searched (only six *benchmark* resorts were named, implying
  acceptable luxury tier/region, not an exhaustive destination list).

To produce a defensible, verifiable shortlist rather than a purely theoretical one, the following
assumptions were made and **must be reconfirmed with Wayfair before this report is treated as final**:

- **Travel dates used for pricing verification:** October 18–23, 2027 (5 nights, double occupancy).
  This is a shoulder-season window (avoiding peak winter holiday surcharges) chosen only so that a
  concrete, checkable price could be pulled from each resort's official website. **Actual Wayfair travel
  dates will change pricing and must be re-verified before contracting.**
- **Geographic scope:** Search was limited to the countries represented by the six named benchmark
  resorts — **Dominican Republic, Mexico, Barbados, and Turkey** — since these are the only countries
  explicitly referenced in the brief (via the benchmark properties). Per the mandatory restrictions,
  Africa, Russia, and the Philippines were excluded outright, and no other country was added
  speculatively. **If Wayfair intends a different or broader destination scope, this report must be
  re-run against that explicit list.**
- **Room type priced:** Standard/entry luxury suite category, 2 adults, to test the $2,500 total/room
  ceiling. Higher room categories were not priced and may exceed budget.

## 3. Research Methodology

1. Built a candidate list of adults-only, all-inclusive, 5-star-marketed resorts in the four countries above.
2. Verified every mandatory requirement directly against each resort's **official website only** —
   never OTAs (Expedia, Booking.com, Hotels.com, Travelocity) or third-party wholesalers.
3. Eliminated any resort that failed even one mandatory requirement, with the specific reason documented.
4. Scored only the resorts that survived every mandatory gate.
5. Where a fact could not be verified from an official source (pricing, restaurant count, transfer time,
   renovation status, adults-only enforcement, event capability), it is labeled **UNVERIFIED** rather than
   estimated — and per the brief's Confidence Standard, an unverified mandatory requirement results in
   elimination, not a benefit-of-the-doubt pass.

## 10. Final Executive Test

For each recommended resort:

1. Would this resort be appropriate if Wayfair's CEO attended?
2. Would it impress someone accustomed to Four Seasons / Ritz-Carlton / Aman / Rosewood?
3. Would attendees likely share photos because the resort feels exceptional?
4. Does this experience clearly exceed a typical Marriott / Hilton / Hyatt?
5. Would you personally recommend spending more than $150,000 USD of Wayfair's budget here?

If any answer is "No," the reason is stated explicitly rather than omitted.
