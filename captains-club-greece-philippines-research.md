# Captains Club research: Greece and the Philippines

**Dates:** May 16–19, 2027 (3 nights)  
**Group:** 16 rooms / approximately 32 adults  
**Budget:** USD $2,500 all-in per room for the stay  
**Gates applied:** officially marketed 5-star; adults-only; true all-inclusive (meals **and** beverages, including premium spirits and wines, in the rate — not optional packages, meal credits, or limited-hour plans); 3+ à la carte restaurants (buffets do not count); luxury comparable to Secrets / Impression / Sandals Royal Barbados / Excellence / Valentin Imperial / Maxx Royal; ≤40 minutes by road from an international airport; dedicated groups/events capability; modern suites; no boat/ferry/seaplane/helicopter arrival.

**Headline:** Neither country produced a resort that passes every mandatory gate. Official May 2027 group pricing is unverified in every case.

If one gate is later relaxed, the order below is the commercially honest shortlist — not a qualifying ranking.

---

## Greece

Greece has many 5-star adults-only hotels and many large all-inclusive resorts. The combination **adults-only + true all-inclusive + 3 à la carte restaurants + ≤40-minute airport transfer + Secrets-level luxury** almost never exists on the same property. The luxury all-inclusives (Ikos, Grecotel LUXME, Mitsis Selection) are family resorts. The adults-only hotels near airports are usually half-board or limited-hour / rationed all-inclusive.

### Strongest near-matches (not fully qualified)

| Rank | Property | Closest gates passed | Controlling fail |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **King Jason Zante**, Tsilivi, Zakynthos | Official 5-star; adults-only; lifestyle all-inclusive as the product (premium cocktails, branded spirits, wine cellar, daily minibar); conference room to 80; 8 km from Zakynthos International (ZTH) | Only **two** specialty à la carte restaurants (Pisco Peruvian Fusion and Haroūb Levantine), and they **rotate nightly** — one is open each evening. Watergarden is the all-day main restaurant with buffet / show-cooking. That fails the 3-à-la-carte gate on a 3-night stay. Luxury is a strong lifestyle product, not Maxx Royal / Secrets scale. Drive time is implied by 8 km, not an official minute count. |
| 2 | **The Ixian Grand & All Suites**, Ixia, Rhodes | Official 5-star adults-only beachfront; marketed as all-inclusive; three named à la carte venues (Milonges Greek, Muza Htista Japanese-Peruvian, Alazonia suites restaurant); 7 km / typically ~15 minutes from Rhodes Diagoras (RHO); 348 rooms | Official homepage itself rations à la carte: **“Two a la carte dinner per stay (min.stay 7)”**. A 3-night Captains Club stay would not get unrestricted dining. Alazonia is suite-only. All-inclusive is described as “sunrise to sunset,” not 24-hour true AI. Third-party operator copy still lists half-board / full-board, so AI may not be the only product. |
| 3 | **Ikos Aria**, Kefalos, Kos | Closest luxury and dining match in Greece: Infinite Lifestyle all-inclusive; **7 à la carte + 1 buffet**; premium wines; 24-hour room service; Dine Out; dedicated events/groups language; **official 20 minutes from Kos Airport (KGS)** | **Family resort.** Kids’ clubs, kids’ pools, splash pad, children’s menus. ### If one gate is dropped (Greece)

- Drop **adults-only** → **Ikos Aria, Kos** is the only property that also clears luxury, true AI, dining, and the 20-minute airport gate. Negotiate Deluxe Collection / adults-only pool block for 16 rooms.
- Drop **3 à la carte** → **King Jason Zante** is the only property that also clears adults-only + true lifestyle AI + short airport road. Ask Groups to run both specialty restaurants every night for the block, or add a private third dinner.
- Keep every gate → **no qualifying Greece resort.**

## Philippines

Road-access 5-star beach inventory sits almost entirely on **Mactan (CEB)**. Boracay, El Nido, Coron, Siargao, and Amanpulo fail the non-road transfer rule. There is **no** official 5-star, adults-only, true all-inclusive resort on a 40-minute road from an international airport.

### Strongest near-matches (not fully qualified)

| Rank | Property | Closest gates passed | Controlling fail |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Plantation Bay Resort & Spa**, Mactan, Cebu | Official 5-star destination resort; **Destination Cebu Package** is a real all-inclusive: unlimited restaurant food (imported meats excluded) plus unlimited standard wine, beer, spirits, and non-alcoholic drinks; four restaurants (Kilimanjaro Kafe, Fiji, Palermo, Route 66 Diner); official **~30 minutes to CEB** (some listings ~11 minutes / 5.7 km) | **Family resort** (children’s programs, children’s menu, water slides / lagoon play). Drink service is capped (“about 3 drinks per two hours”). Imported meats extra. Not Secrets / Impression luxury. Groups capability must be confirmed in writing. |
| 2 | **Shangri-La Mactan, Cebu** | Official 5-star; ~20 minutes from CEB; 7 restaurants; 541 rooms; dedicated events; closest luxury bar in the country to the Captains Club benchmark | **Family** (child-friendly facilities). Standard board is **breakfast**, not true AI. |
| 3 | **Dusit Thani Mactan Cebu** | 5-star beach resort; events centre; spa; road access from CEB | Fun Zone kids club; babysitting; not adults-only; not true AI. |

### If one gate is dropped (Philippines)

- Drop **adults-only** → **Plantation Bay** is the only Mactan property with a documented true (if capped) all-inclusive package, four restaurants, and a short CEB transfer. Confirm king bedding, a quiet-block, and whether the drink cap / imported-meat exclusions can be waived for the group.
- Drop **true all-inclusive** → **Shangri-La Mactan** is the only property at the executive luxury bar, with events infrastructure and a 20-minute CEB transfer. All F&B would be a negotiated buyout, not an AI rate.
- Keep every gate → **no qualifying Philippines resort.**

## What this means for RFPs

Do not send the standard Captains Club RFP expecting a clean pass in either country.

**Greece — send RFPs only if leadership accepts a written waiver:**

1. **Ikos Aria (Kos)** — waiver: adults-only. Ask for Deluxe Collection / adults-only pool and beach for 16 rooms, private welcome and farewell, and Infinite Lifestyle inclusions in writing.
2. **King Jason Zante** — waiver: third à la carte restaurant. Ask Groups to operate both Pisco and Haroūb nightly plus a private third dinner, and to confirm ZTH drive time ≤40 minutes for coaches.
3. **The Ixian Grand (Rhodes)** — waiver: true unrestricted AI dining. Ask for unlimited à la carte for the block (not two dinners per 7-night stay), premium spirits in writing, and a 3-night group board that is not half-board.

**Philippines — send RFPs only if leadership accepts a written waiver:**

1. **Plantation Bay** — waiver: adults-only, plus confirmation that drink caps and meat exclusions are lifted.
2. **Shangri-La Mactan** — waiver: true all-inclusive (replace with a full F&B buyout).

May 2027 pricing remains **pending official group proposal** in every case. None of the above may be ranked or scored as a qualifying Captains Club resort until the waived gate is approved in writing and Path B pricing is in hand.
