# Hotel Riu Sri Lanka — destination briefing

Program dates: May 16–19, 2027 | 16 rooms / ~32 adults  
Primary sources: RIU official hotel/gastronomy pages and fact sheet (2025); Sri Lanka Department of Immigration & Emigration / ETA portal (effective 25 May 2026); U.S. State Department Sri Lanka Travel Advisory (14 October 2025); Sri Lanka Department of Meteorology (May 2026 southwest monsoon notices); destination excursion operators.  
May 2027 group pricing is not verified.

## Location

- **Resort:** Hotel Riu Sri Lanka, Ahungalla Beach, Galle District, south coast, beachfront on the Indian Ocean (Laccadive Sea).
- **Setting:** Long beach resort belt between Bentota and Hikkaduwa. Quieter than Colombo; less built-up than Negombo.
- **Airport:** Bandaranaike International Airport (CMB), Colombo. **~120–130 km.** Typical private-car drive is about **2 to 2.5 hours** via the Southern Expressway (E01) and Bentota exit. **This fails the 40-minute transfer gate.** Confirm the exact group transfer time in the proposal.
- **Closest town:** Ahungalla ~500 m. **Bentota** ~15 km. **Colombo** ~90 km. **Galle** ~50 km.
- **Inventory:** 500+ rooms. RIU does not publish a groups fact sheet. Confirm whether an Elite Club block exists here and whether 16 rooms can be isolated within it.
- **Opened:** 2022. May 2027 is within normal operations.

## Safety

- **U.S. State Department (14 October 2025): Sri Lanka Level 2 — Exercise Increased Caution** due to civil unrest, terrorism, and landmines. The landmine risk is in northern/eastern districts, not the southwest coast.
- Ahungalla/Bentota is a resort strip whose economy depends on tourism. Tourist Police have a visible presence. Main risks: petty theft, card/ATM fraud, overcharging, occasional tout scams. Violent crime against tourists is rare.
- Avoid isolated beaches at night and demonstrations. Secure valuables in the in-room safe.
- Emergency: Police **119**, Tourist Police **119/011-242-1451**, Ambulance **110/1990**. U.S. Embassy Colombo: ColomboACS@state.gov.
- Medical: local clinics in Ahungalla/Bentota; nearest major hospital is in Balapitiya or Aluthgama. Most hotels have a doctor on call. Confirm clinic and hospital transfer time in the RFP.
- Bentota/Ahungalla beaches can have strong currents in the southwest monsoon. Swim where lifeguards/flags indicate; use hotel pools.

## Weather in May

- Ahungalla is in the **southwest/wet zone**. May is the **wettest month** of the year here as the southwest monsoon establishes.
- Typical: **29–31°C / 85–88°F** days, **25–27°C / 77–81°F** nights, high humidity. Heat index (“feels like”) often **36–39°C / 97–102°F**.
- Rainfall: about **300–340 mm** in May, with roughly **16–28 rainy days**. Showers/thunderstorms, often afternoon. Not constant all-day rain, but outdoor events need an indoor backup.
- Department of Meteorology confirmed the monsoon was establishing over the island on **23–26 May 2026**, with heavy falls (~100 mm) possible in Galle and Matara districts (the Ahungalla coast sits in this belt).
- Sea state on the south/southwest coast is rougher from May. Build a weather contingency into any boat day.

## Entry requirements (US / China)

Confirm with Sri Lanka Immigration / the relevant embassy immediately before travel. Rules below are those in force from **25 May 2026**.

**Both nationalities**
- Obtain an **Electronic Travel Authorization (ETA)** at [eta.gov.lk](https://www.eta.gov.lk/slvisa/) **before arrival**. For the 40 listed countries (including US and China) the ETA is **free** and valid for **30 days, double entry**.
- Passport valid **at least 6 months** from arrival, with a blank page.
- Return/onward ticket and proof of sufficient funds.
- A 3-night stay is well inside the 30-day ETA.

**United States (ordinary passport)**  
Free 30-day tourist ETA under the 40-country scheme. No visa fee under this scheme.

**China (ordinary / official / service / diplomatic passport)**  
Free 30-day tourist ETA under the same 40-country scheme. Double entry within the 30-day validity.

Reconfirm both policies in spring 2027. Do not treat this briefing as immigration advice.

## Land excursions (executive-appropriate, from Ahungalla)

Private air-conditioned vehicles through the resort or a vetted operator. Drive times are typical.

1. **Galle Dutch Fort** — UNESCO World Heritage site, ramparts, colonial streets, lighthouse. About 50 km south.
2. **Madu Ganga river safari** — mangrove boat safari, cinnamon islands, fish spa, birdlife. Close to Ahungalla/Bentota.
3. **Kosgoda turtle hatchery** — sea turtle conservation; release season runs into May.
4. **Lunuganga and Brief Garden** — Geoffrey Bawa and Bevis Bawa estates; the signature Sri Lankan landscape-architecture visit.

Also nearby: Ariyapala Mask Museum (Ambalangoda), moonstone mine (Meetiyagoda), Tsunami Museum. Skip Colombo nightlife for this group.

## Sea excursions

**Seasonality caveat:** Mirissa whale watching’s main season is **November–April**. May 16–19 sits at the **tail end / out of season**, with rougher seas as the monsoon establishes. Treat whale watching as weather-dependent and not guaranteed.

1. **Mirissa whale and dolphin watching** — blue whales, sperm whales, dolphins. Early start (~4–5 am pickup), boat ~6:30 am, 4–5 hours. Only with a weather-cancel clause; sightings not guaranteed in May.
2. **Bentota river / mangrove boat** — sheltered inland waterway; lower weather risk than open ocean. Good backup sea day.
3. **Bentota beach water sports** — jet skis, banana boats, kayaks. Use licensed hotel-approved operators only; avoid unlicensed beach vendors.
4. **Sunset river cruise** — sheltered estuary; fallback if daytime ocean trips are cancelled.

Do not include unlicensed jet-ski rentals. Treat open-ocean boat trips as cancelable.
