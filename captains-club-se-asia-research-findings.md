# Captains Club SE Asia research findings

**Dates:** May 16–19, 2027 (3 nights)  
**Group:** 16 rooms / approximately 32 adults  
**Budget:** USD $2,500 all-in per room for the stay  
**Gates applied:** 5-star marketed; adults-only; true all-inclusive; 3+ à la carte restaurants (buffets excluded); comparable luxury to Secrets / Impression / Sandals Royal Barbados / Excellence / Valentin Imperial / Maxx Royal; ≤40 minutes by road from an international airport; dedicated groups/events capability; modern suites; no boat/ferry/seaplane/helicopter arrival.

**Headline:** None of the five destinations produced two fully qualifying resorts. Official May 2027 group pricing is unverified in every case.

## Thailand

| Rank | Property | Pass | Fail |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The Shore at Katathani, Kata Noi, Phuket | Adults-only (18+); luxury pool villas; Harbor, Param Para, and Orora as dining venues; events/weddings | Not true all-inclusive (packages/meal plans, not standard board); HKT to Kata Noi is typically 45–60 minutes / ~34 km |
| 2 | SAii Koh Samui Villas, Hanuman Bay | Adults-oriented pool-villa resort; ~7–10 minutes from USM; beach access; buyout capability | Official FAQ allows guests 12+ (not a true 18+ adults-only policy); breakfast-led, not true AI; two on-site restaurants plus virtual kitchens (virtual kitchens do not count); beach club / day-visitor model; luxury bar below Secrets / Maxx Royal |

Also eliminated: Riu Palace Phuket (true AI, family, kids pool, opens Sept 2026); Renaissance Phuket (optional AI, family); Aleenta Phuket Phang Nga (~25 min from HKT, family packages, not AI); Layana Koh Lanta (adults-only, boat/long transfer); Rixos Water World Phuket (family waterpark, 2028).

## Philippines

Road-access 5-star inventory concentrates on Mactan (CEB). Boracay, El Nido, Siargao, and Amanpulo fail the non-road transfer rule.

| Rank | Property | Pass | Fail |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Shangri-La Mactan, Cebu | Official 5-star; ~20 minutes from CEB; 7 restaurants; 541 rooms; dedicated events; modern rooms | Family resort with child-friendly facilities; standard board is breakfast, not true AI |
| 2 | Dusit Thani Mactan Cebu | 5-star beach resort; events centre; spa; road access from CEB | Fun Zone kids club; babysitting; not adults-only; not true AI |

Also eliminated: Crimson Mactan (Crimzone family water play); Mövenpick Mactan (Little Birds Club); Discovery Shores / Shangri-La Boracay (boat from Caticlan); Nay Palad Hideaway (Siargao, not adults-only, childcare language); Amanpulo (private aircraft).

## Cambodia — Siem Reap

The new Siem Reap Angkor International Airport (SAI) is ~45–50 km from town. Shinta Mani’s official contact page states a **45-minute** drive. Independent transfer guides put central hotels at **45–60 minutes**. That fails the 40-minute gate for the destination as a whole.

| Rank | Property | Pass | Fail |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Raffles Grand Hotel d’Angkor | 5-star heritage luxury; 1932 + Café d’Angkor + Poolside Terrace; meetings (Kravan, Marquee); butler service | Not adults-only; not true AI; SAI transfer typically 45–60 minutes |
| 2 | Shinta Mani Angkor (Bensley Collection) | Distinctive design; Kroya dining; butler; incentive-scale buyout possible | Not adults-only; not true AI (AI language is for Shinta Mani Wild / combo packages); official 45-minute SAI transfer |

Also eliminated: Anantara Angkor (optional “Angkor All-Inclusive Luxury” package, not a true AI resort; 39 suites; pet-friendly); Park Hyatt Siem Reap (city hotel, families, not AI, same SAI transfer); WAKA Villa (16+ but villa-with-kitchenette, not 5-star AI, not 32-guest events scale).

## Sri Lanka

Only the Negombo / airport belt reliably sits inside 40 minutes of CMB.

| Rank | Property | Pass | Fail |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Jetwing Lagoon Wellness, Negombo | Official adults-only 5-star; Geoffrey Bawa architecture; ~10–20 minutes from CMB; meeting rooms | Wellness / breakfast-led, not true AI; alcohol is understated; Blue Lagoon is the primary restaurant (does not clearly present 3 à la carte restaurants); lagoon-front, not a Caribbean-style beach AI |
| 2 | Jetwing Blue, Negombo | Official 5-star; minutes from CMB; ballroom to 800; dining variety | Family rooms; kids facilities; not true AI |

Also eliminated: Sentido Heritance Negombo (family, kids pool, AI is an add-on); Hotel Riu Sri Lanka Ahungalla (true 24-hour AI but family, RiuLand, ~2 hours from CMB); Sun Siyam Pasikudah (24-hour AI but family kids club, 5–6 hours or seaplane); Santani Wellness (12+, full-board wellness, ~3 hours from CMB); Thaulle (Ayurveda, 13+, far south).

## What this means for RFPs

Do not send the current Captains Club RFP unchanged and expect compliant proposals. Either keep the gates and drop these five destinations, or explicitly waive **adults-only** and **true all-inclusive** for Asia and score a different product: 5-star luxury, road access, dining depth, and event execution, with a negotiated dining credit or full-board buyout.
