# Wayfair Captains Club Resort Research Framework

## Purpose

Identify luxury, adults-only, true all-inclusive resorts appropriate for Wayfair's executive recognition program. The program is designed for approximately 30 attendees in 15 rooms. Recommendations must be defensible to Executive Leadership and Procurement; quality and guest experience take precedence over list length or lowest price.

## Research Sources

Use only:

- The resort's official website, including its official accommodation, dining, and meetings/events pages.
- The resort's official direct-booking engine.
- Written proposals sent by the resort's official Groups, Meetings, Events, or Sales department.

Do not use third-party travel agencies, online travel agencies, wholesalers, review sites, or unverified search snippets as evidence.

## Stage 1: Mandatory Qualification

Eliminate a resort if any applicable requirement cannot be verified from an approved source.

### Location and access

- Search only the countries and destinations approved by the requester.
- Exclude destinations or properties requiring a boat, ferry, seaplane, or helicopter transfer.
- Verify that the property is reachable entirely by road from an international airport in 40 minutes or less. Record the verified drive time and source.
- Confirm stable tourism infrastructure, reliable ground transportation, and access to quality medical services.

### Resort and accommodation

- The resort must be officially marketed as a five-star resort, adults-only, and true all-inclusive.
- Standard booking must include food and beverages. Exclude optional all-inclusive, meal-plan, resort-credit, half-board, full-board, and breakfast-only products.
- Exclude family resorts, waterpark resorts, and properties primarily designed for children.
- Require a minimum of four à la carte restaurants; buffets do not count.
- Verify modern, executive-appropriate suites with contemporary design, premium bedding, luxury finishes, and modern bathrooms. Exclude visibly dated or worn accommodations.
- Require a dedicated Meetings, Groups, Events, or Sales team capable of professionally delivering a welcome reception, cocktail reception, awards ceremony, and farewell dinner for 30 guests.

### Experience threshold

The property must meet or exceed the executive feel, service, dining, privacy, and accommodation quality of the approved luxury benchmark set. Do not recommend a resort merely because it technically passes the criteria.

## Pricing: Two-Path Official Verification

Consumer retail inventory is not the controlling commercial benchmark for a 15-room corporate program. A resort may pass price verification through either path below. Third-party pricing is never acceptable.

### Path A: Official direct-booking engine

Use the exact requested dates, room count, occupancy, and a qualifying standard suite (or closest qualifying suite). Capture:

- Resort and room category
- Check-in and check-out dates
- Guest count and room count
- Currency and conversion method, if applicable
- Nightly rate, taxes and mandatory fees
- Three-night total per room
- Booking URL and timestamp
- Screenshot or other reproducible evidence of the official result

### Path B: Official group quote

For 10 or more rooms, a written proposal from the resort's official Groups, Meetings, Events, or Sales department is acceptable and controls over retail pricing. The proposal must identify:

- Resort, proposal date, contact name, title, and official company email domain
- Exact travel dates and 15 rooms / 30 adults
- Room category and bedding configuration
- Nightly rate, taxes, fees, resort charges, and the all-in three-night total per room
- Included all-inclusive food and beverage terms
- Private-event charges, food-and-beverage minimums, and mandatory group charges
- Inventory hold, proposal expiration, payment, attrition, and cancellation terms
- Group concessions and upgrades, if any
- Construction, renovation, or operational-disruption disclosure

The resort passes the price gate only when its standard qualifying suite is at or below the user-provided all-in per-room maximum for the full stay. If a price is unavailable, undated, based on another travel period, or excludes mandatory charges, mark it **unverified** and eliminate it from the final ranking.

## Workflow

### Phase 1: Qualification shortlist

1. Build a comprehensive candidate list within the approved geography.
2. Apply every mandatory location, resort, accommodation, dining, event, and experience criterion.
3. Preserve source URLs and quoted evidence for each passing candidate.
4. Mark pricing as **verified via direct booking**, **verified via official group quote**, or **pending official verification**.
5. Do not score or recommend a resort with pending pricing.

### Phase 2: Commercial validation

For every Phase 1 candidate with pricing pending, issue an identical request for proposal through the official Groups, Meetings, Events, or Sales contact. Request:

> Wayfair Captains Club: May 16–19, 2027; 15 rooms / 30 adults; three nights. Please quote the closest qualifying standard suite and one upgraded-suite option. Include all taxes, mandatory fees, resort charges, food-and-beverage minimums, private-event costs, group concessions, attrition and cancellation terms, construction disclosures, and availability for a welcome reception, cocktail reception, awards ceremony, and farewell dinner.

Do not treat a consumer rate as an indication that the group program will be available, contractable, or within budget.

## Stage 2: Evaluation and Ranking

Rank only resorts that pass every Stage 1 criterion and one pricing-verification path. Score each category from 1 to 10 and provide a written explanation with official evidence:

- Luxury experience
- Executive atmosphere
- Service
- Food
- Event capability
- Relaxation and atmosphere
- Excursions
- Airport accessibility
- Value

Use the official all-in group quote when assessing value for a 15-room program. Clearly separate room cost from event and ancillary costs.

## Red Flags and Executive Test

State material concerns for every recommended resort, including construction, seasonal crowding, noise, beach conditions, service inconsistency, limited excursions, restrictive event terms, or any qualification caveat.

Before recommending a resort, answer:

1. Would it be appropriate if Wayfair's CEO attended?
2. Would it impress guests accustomed to Four Seasons, Ritz-Carlton, Aman, or Rosewood?
3. Would guests likely share photos because the experience feels exceptional?
4. Does it clearly exceed a typical Marriott, Hilton, or Hyatt stay?
5. Would the consultant recommend Wayfair spend more than $150,000 at the property?

If any answer is no, explain why and do not obscure the concern.

## Final Deliverable

Provide only the ranked resorts that passed all gates. For each recommendation, include official evidence from the resort, accommodation, dining, and meetings/events pages; the verified pricing record; category scores and rationales; red flags; and executive-test answers.

If no resort has verified pricing, return no recommendations. Explain that the qualification shortlisting may continue, but commercial validation is required before a property can be presented as an executive recommendation.
