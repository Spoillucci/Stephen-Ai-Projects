# Captains Club Group RFP Email Template

Use this template for first-contact outreach to a resort’s official Groups, Meetings, Events, or Sales team. Send from a `@wayfair.com` address. Fill every `[bracketed]` field before sending. Keep the numbered proposal list intact so replies map to the research framework.

**Sender notes**

- Replace `[Proposal due date]`, `[Shortlist date]`, and `[Decision date]` with a realistic internal calendar. Do not send without dates.
- Use the same dates, room count, occupancy, and event list in every RFP so proposals are comparable.
- If a property replies that it cannot meet the qualification filter, close the inquiry. Do not negotiate those criteria.

---

**Subject:** Wayfair — Captains Club 2027 Group RFP | 15 rooms, May 16–19

Dear [Name / Groups & Incentives Team],

Wayfair (NYSE: W) is one of the world’s largest online destinations for home goods, headquartered in Boston, MA. I am reaching out regarding Captains Club, our invitation-only executive recognition trip for top-performing employees, senior leaders, and their spouses or partners. Guests are executive-level, with high service expectations and low tolerance for friction.

Captains Club is an annual recognition program; this engagement is the first of potentially recurring bookings. We are evaluating a small number of luxury resorts that can deliver a quiet, high-end experience and professionally host our private events. [Resort Name] is on our shortlist.

**To be considered, the property must be:**

- Officially marketed as adults-only
- True all-inclusive: all food and beverages — including premium spirits and wines — included in the room rate; not a meal-credit, half-board, full-board, or breakfast-only plan
- Home to at least four à la carte restaurants (buffets do not count)
- Within approximately 40 minutes by road of an international airport
- Equipped with a dedicated Groups, Meetings, Events, or Sales team capable of professionally executing private events for approximately 30 guests

If [Resort Name] does not meet these criteria, please reply to that effect so we can close this inquiry.

**Program details**

- Dates: May 16–19, 2027 (3 nights)
- Rooms: 15 rooms / approximately 30 adults (2 adults per room)
- Occupancy: Adults only
- Board: True all-inclusive, as defined above
- Events: Welcome reception, cocktail reception, awards ceremony, and farewell dinner for approximately 30 guests
- Preferred room type: Closest qualifying standard suite, plus one upgraded-suite option

**Please confirm in a written proposal**

1. Availability for these dates and a 15-room block
2. Room category, bedding (king preferred), nightly rate, and all-in three-night total per room
3. Whether taxes, service charges, and mandatory resort fees are included
4. All-inclusive inclusions and any exclusions, using the definition above
5. Private-event venues, food-and-beverage minimums, AV, staffing, porterage, and other mandatory group charges
6. Deposit, attrition, cancellation, and proposal expiration terms
7. Construction or operational-disruption disclosures for May 2027
8. Approximate transfer time from the nearest international airport
9. Offsite excursion options and local partnerships suitable for approximately 30 executives and spouses
10. One or two references for similar-sized corporate incentive groups hosted in the past 24 months

Our lodging target is USD $2,500 or less per room for the full three-night stay, all-in. Value and inclusions are weighed alongside price; please propose your best-fit package rather than the lowest stripped rate.

Please submit proposals by **[Proposal due date]**. We expect to shortlist by **[Shortlist date]** and confirm by **[Decision date]**. I would welcome a 20-minute discovery call ahead of your proposal.

All agreements will be subject to Wayfair’s standard vendor/procurement process.

If [Resort Name] can host Captains Club, please reply with a group proposal and the best Groups, Meetings, or Sales contact for next steps.
