## Travel safety advisory

- **U.S. State Department (23 October 2025): Greece Level 1 — Exercise Normal Precautions.** The lowest advisory level. Greece is “generally safe”; petty crime (pickpocketing, purse-snatching) is the main issue, especially at tourist sites and on public transport. Demonstrations can occur with little notice.
- Kos specifically: composite safety index ~82/100; violent crime against tourists is rare. Main risks are pickpocketing in Kos Town/harbour areas, tourist-targeted scams, scooter/quad accidents, nightlife overcharging, heat exposure and seasonal wildfire risk.
- Earthquake/wildfire note: Greece is seismically active; Kos has experienced earthquakes. Check local fire-risk maps in May. May is not peak wildfire season, but heat can build late month.
- Emergency: **112** for police/ambulance/fire. U.S. Embassy Athens: +30-210-721-2951. Tourist Police are present in Kos Town.
- Medical: Kos has a main hospital in Kos Town; private clinics available. Confirm doctor/hospital access from the resort in the RFP.
- Practical: secure valuables in the in-room safe; agree taxi fares in advance; avoid isolated beaches at night; stay hydrated.

## Weather in May

- May is one of the best months for Kos: warm but not yet peak-summer hot, low rainfall, long daylight.
- Official Kos station (May 2026): mean high **23.8°C / 75°F**, mean low **16.1°C / 61°F**, mean **19.9°C / 68°F**. Total rainfall **44.8 mm** for the month, wettest day 40 mm on 23 May. Wind avg 12.1 km/h (NNW). Light rain/thunderstorm days are occasional, not constant.
- Forecast model for May: highs ~22°C, lows ~17°C, ~20 mm rainfall over ~6 rainy days, 25 dry days. Comfortable for outdoor events; an indoor backup is still wise.
- **Sea temperature:** ~17–19°C in May. Brisk — fine for quick dips and active swimming; pack a 2–3 mm spring suit for longer swims.
- **Sailing/wind:** Kos sits in a moderately windy Dodecanese zone — less severe than the central Cyclades Meltemi, more exposed than the Ionian. The Meltemi begins May but is strongest mid-July to mid-August, so mid-May is on the early edge. Boat days are very feasible; check the marine forecast and keep a weather-flexible window.

## Entry requirements (US / China)

Confirm with the Greek MFA / EU sources immediately before travel. Rules below are those in force in 2026.

**United States (ordinary passport)**
- **Visa-free** for tourism/business up to **90 days in any rolling 180-day period** across the Schengen Area.
- Passport issued within the last 10 years and valid at least **3 months beyond** planned departure from Schengen.
- **EES (Entry/Exit System):** biometric registration at the external Schengen border on arrival — no advance application.
- **ETIAS:** not yet operational as of 2026; possible late-2026/2027 launch. Recheck the official EU site before 2027 travel; if live, a pre-travel electronic authorisation will be required.

**China (ordinary passport)**
- **Schengen visa required** before travel. China has no visa-waiver agreement with the EU.
- Apply in person at a Greek Visa Application Centre (GVCW) in China; biometric appointment; decision ~15 business days. Type C short-stay visa, up to 90 days in 180.
- Passport: at least two blank pages, valid at least 3 months beyond departure from Schengen.
- Build visa lead time into the Captains Club timeline for the Shanghai-based guests.

A 3-night stay is well within the 90-day limit for both nationalities.

## Sea excursions (executive-appropriate, from Kos)

May is on the early edge of the Meltemi; boat days are feasible but should be booked with a weather-cancel/flexible clause. Use vetted operators through the resort.

1. **Three-island day cruise (Pserimos, Kalymnos, Platy)** — full-day boat from Kos Port; swimming/snorkelling stops, Greek lunch on Kalymnos. ~8 hours. The signature Kos boat day.
2. **Nisyros volcano island** — full-day cruise from Kardamena to Mandraki, then bus to Nikia and the **Stefanos crater** (active volcano, UNESCO Geopark). Walk the crater rim, feel the geothermal heat. The standout “only-in-Kos” excursion.
3. **Kalymnos — sponge-diving island & Vathy sea cave** — quieter option; Pothia town, sponge heritage, swim at Rina/Vathy.
4. **Private catamaran / yacht charter** — half-day or sunset cruise along the Kos coast; most executive-appropriate; book exclusively for the group. Confirm sea state and wind forecast.

Treat shared large-boat excursions as weather-flexible; the private catamaran is the cleanest fit for 30 executives.

## Land excursions (executive-appropriate, from Kos)

1. **Asklepion (Asklepieion)** — ancient healing temple of Hippocrates, ~4 km from Kos Town; the island’s signature cultural site. Combine with a guided Hippocrates/Garden of Life visit.
2. **Kos Town & Medieval/Ottoman quarter** — harbour, Castle of the Knights, Plane Tree of Hippocrates, markets. Easy half-day.
3. **Antimachia Castle & Zia village** — Venetian-era castle; Zia hilltop village for sunset. Combine for a half-day.
4. **Local Drive Adventure (included)** — complimentary electric MINI Countryman for a self-guided island day; wineries, vineyards, ancient ruins. A premium, low-friction option for couples.

Cycling is popular on Kos (flat network to Asklepion/Lambi), but for a 30-executive group, private vehicles/guides are cleaner than a self-guided bike day.
