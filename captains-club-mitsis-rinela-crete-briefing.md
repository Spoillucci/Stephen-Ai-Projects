## Location

- **Resort:** Mitsis Selection Rinela, Kokkini Hani, north coast of Crete, Heraklion region, Greece.
- **Setting:** Beachfront on a Blue Flag beach in Kokkini Hani, ~650 m from the village centre and **14 km east of Heraklion**. Vibrant small resort village; close to Creta Aquarium (2 km) and Watercity waterpark (1 km).
- **Airport:** Heraklion International Airport “Nikos Kazantzakis” (HER). **~10–11 km, typically 12–15 minutes by private car/coach.** **Passes the 40-minute transfer gate comfortably** — one of the shortest transfers in the entire research run. (Chania/CHQ is ~160 km / ~2 h 20 min — do not route via CHQ.)
- **Inventory:** 393 renovated rooms, suites and villas; private/sharing pool rooms available. A 16-room block is realistic; request a villa/private-pool cluster for the executive feel.
- **Opened/Renovated:** Originally 20th-century operation; rooms, suites, villas and public spaces recently renovated. Modern finishes — no dated-room concern.

## Travel safety advisory

- **U.S. State Department (23 October 2025): Greece Level 1 — Exercise Normal Precautions** (lowest level). Greece is “generally safe”; petty crime (pickpocketing, purse-snatching) at tourist sites and on public transport is the main issue. Demonstrations can occur with little notice.
- Crete is generally low-risk for tourists; violent crime is rare. Main risks: pickpocketing in Heraklion/market areas, tourist scams, scooter/quad accidents, nightlife overcharging, heat exposure, and seasonal wildfire risk (May is early for wildfires but heat can build late month). Greece is seismically active.
- Emergency: **112** for police/ambulance/fire. U.S. Embassy Athens +30-210-721-2951. Resort has a 24-hour doctor on call (extra charge) and a doctor inside the hotel; General Hospital 12 km; private clinic 4 km; pharmacy opposite the hotel. Lifeguard by pools and beach.
- Practical: secure valuables in the in-room safe; agree taxi fares in advance; avoid isolated beaches at night; stay hydrated.

## Weather in May

- May is one of the best months for Crete: warm but not peak-summer hot, low rainfall, long daylight, sea warming through the month.
- Heraklion May averages: high **24°C / 75°F**, low **15–17°C / 59–63°F**, mean **~20°C / 68°F**. Rainfall low — ~15–32 mm over ~2–4 rainy days. ~14 h daylight, ~9.7 h sunshine, humidity ~58–61%, wind ~13 km/h.
- **Sea temperature:** ~19–20°C average (17°C early May → ~22°C by month-end). Brisk/cool — swimmable especially late May; pack a 2–3 mm spring suit for longer swims. The resort beach is sandy/pebbly and lifeguard-attended.
- Outdoor events and excursions are very feasible in May; an indoor backup remains wise. No monsoon concern here — May is dry-season shoulder, far better than the May monsoon profile of Sri Lanka/Phuket.

## Entry requirements (US / China)

Confirm with the Greek MFA / EU sources immediately before travel. Rules below are those in force in 2026.

**United States (ordinary passport)**
- **Visa-free** for tourism/business up to **90 days in any rolling 180-day period** across the Schengen Area.
- Passport issued within the last 10 years and valid at least **3 months beyond** planned departure from Schengen.
- **EES (Entry/Exit System):** biometric registration at the external Schengen border on arrival — no advance application.
- **ETIAS:** not yet operational as of 2026; possible late-2026/2027 launch. Recheck the official EU site before 2027 travel; if live, a pre-travel electronic authorisation will be required.

**China (ordinary passport)**
- **Schengen visa required** before travel. China has no visa-waiver agreement with the EU.
- Apply in person at a Greek Visa Application Centre (GVCW) in China; biometric appointment; decision ~15 business days. Type C short-stay visa, up to 90 days in 180.
- Passport: at least two blank pages, valid at least 3 months beyond departure from Schengen.
- Build visa lead time into the Captains Club timeline for the Shanghai-based guests.

A 3-night stay is well within the 90-day limit for both nationalities.

## Sea excursions (executive-appropriate, from Kokkini Hani / Heraklion)

May sea state is calm on the north coast; boat days are very feasible. Use vetted operators through the resort.

1. **Spinalonga Island + Agios Nikolaos + Kolokitha Bay** — full day: bus to Agios Nikolaos, boat across Mirabello Bay to Spinalonga (the “Island of Tears,” leper-colony history), swim stop at Kolokitha Bay, lunch on board. The signature Crete sea day; pickup from Kokkini Hani.
2. **Private catamaran / yacht charter** — half-day or sunset cruise along the north coast / Mirabello Bay; most executive-appropriate; book exclusively for the group. Confirm sea state forecast.
3. **Elounda & Spinalonga (short boat)** — shorter variant: drive to Elounda, short boat to Spinalonga, lunch in Plaka. Lower weather risk than the full Mirabello crossing.
4. **Agia Roumeli boat (combined with Samaria Gorge)** — only for active guests; see land excursions. The boat return from Agia Roumeli to Sougia/Sfakia is the scenic finale.

Treat shared large-boat trips as weather-flexible; the private catamaran is the cleanest fit for 30 executives.

## Land excursions (executive-appropriate, from Kokkini Hani)

1. **Knossos Palace + Heraklion City** — the cradle of European civilisation; 5 km south of Heraklion. Combine with the Heraklion Archaeological Museum (original Minoan frescoes) and the open-air market. The must-do cultural half-day; ~30 min from the resort.
2. **Knossos + Olive Oil + Winery + Raki + Lunch (small-group)** — Knossos self-guided, then olive grove farm tour and tasting, family winery and raki distillery with 5-wine tasting, full traditional lunch at Archanes/Elia village. ~6.5 h; the strongest “taste of Crete” day for executives.
3. **Lasithi Plateau + Cave of Zeus + Traditional Crete** — wind through mountain villages, Vidiani monastery, Krasi village, the Cave of Zeus (birthplace of Zeus, stalactites), olive mill farm. Full day; more cultural/scenic than strenuous.
4. **Samaria Gorge trek (optional, active only)** — Europe’s longest gorge, 18 km trek through 300 m cliffs to Agia Roumeli on the Libyan Sea, then boat to Sougia/Sfakia. Full day, ~14–16 h, strenuous — offer only to the active subset, not the whole group. Most executives should take options 1–3.
