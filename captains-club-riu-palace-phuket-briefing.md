# Hotel Riu Palace Phuket — destination briefing

Program dates: May 16–19, 2027 | 16 rooms / ~32 adults  
Primary sources: RIU official hotel/news pages (Sept 2026); Thai MFA visa scheme effective 15 Sept 2026; U.S. Embassy Bangkok; U.S. State Department Thailand Travel Advisory (7 July 2026); Thai Meteorological Department; Department of National Parks.  
May 2027 group pricing is not verified.

## Location

- Mai Khao Beach, Thalang District, northwest Phuket; beachfront next to Sirinat National Park.
- Phuket International Airport (HKT): 16 km, typically about 20–25 minutes by road. No boat transfer.
- Phuket Old Town: about 40 km.
- 504 rooms; 49 Elite Club rooms. A 16-room block can fit inside Elite Club if contracted.
- Opened 4 September 2026. May 2027 is the hotel’s first May. Require a construction/operational disclosure in the group proposal.

## Safety

- U.S. State Department (7 July 2026): Thailand **Level 1 — Exercise Normal Precautions**. Higher-risk areas (Cambodia border; Yala, Pattani, Narathiwat) do **not** include Phuket.
- Mai Khao is a resort/national-park belt, not a nightlife district. Main tourist risks island-wide: petty theft, motorbike/jet-ski rental scams (do not surrender passports as collateral), inflated bar tabs in Patong, taxi overcharging. Use hotel cars or Grab/Bolt; agree fares or use the meter.
- Emergency: Police **191**, Tourist Police **1155**, Ambulance **1669**.
- Nearest international-standard hospitals are in greater Phuket (Bangkok Hospital Phuket and similar), typically a longer drive than the airport run. Confirm clinic/doctor-on-call and hospital transfer time in the RFP.
- Mai Khao surf and rip currents are often strong, especially in the southwest monsoon. Treat the beach as a walking beach unless the resort posts otherwise; use hotel pools.

## Weather in May

- Southwest monsoon typically begins mid-May. May 16–19 sits at the start of wet season.
- Typical conditions: highs around **30–32°C (86–90°F)**, lows around **25–27°C (77–81°F)**, high humidity, short heavy showers and thunderstorms, often afternoon/evening. Not constant all-day rain, but outdoor plans need indoor backup.
- Thai Meteorological Department reported flooding in Phuket during May 2026 at times. Build weather contingency into sea days.
- Seas on the Andaman west coast are rougher than in December–April.

## Entry requirements (US / China)

Confirm with Thai Immigration / the relevant embassy immediately before travel. Rules below are those in force from **15 September 2026**.

**Both nationalities**
- Complete the free **Thailand Digital Arrival Card (TDAC)** at https://tdac.immigration.go.th within **3 days before arrival**. Not a visa. Avoid third-party paid TDAC sites.
- Passport generally expected to be valid **at least 6 months** from entry, with a blank page.
- Onward/return ticket within the permitted stay; proof of accommodation.
- Immigration may request proof of funds (commonly cited **THB 20,000 per person / 40,000 per family** for visa-exempt tourists). Carry hotel confirmation letters for the group.
- Yellow-fever certificate only if arriving from a risk country.

**United States (ordinary passport)**
- Visa exemption for tourism: **up to 30 days** (reduced from the prior 60-day scheme as of 15 Sept 2026). A 3-night stay is well inside this.
- No visa application required for standard tourism if other conditions are met.

**China (ordinary / public-affairs ordinary passport)**
- Bilateral visa exemption remains in force: **up to 30 days per entry**, cumulative **90 days in any 180-day period**. Tourism and family visits are covered; work, study, journalism, or settlement require a visa in advance.
- Chinese MFA guidance: 6-month passport validity, hotel booking, return ticket, TDAC, cooperate with immigration; do not photograph in restricted airport zones.

Reconfirm both policies in spring 2027. Do not treat this briefing as immigration advice.

## Land excursions (executive-appropriate, from Mai Khao)

Book licensed private vehicles through the resort, not beach touts. Drive times are typical, not verified GPS runs.

1. **Phuket Old Town** — Sino-Portuguese streets, cafés, evening walking dinner. About 40 km.
2. **Big Buddha and Wat Chalong** — island’s principal cultural sites; modest dress required.
3. **Ethical elephant sanctuary (northeast Phuket / Paklok belt)** — observation/walking programs only; no riding. Closer to Mai Khao than south-island attractions.
4. **Golf** — Blue Canyon or Laguna courses; Blue Canyon is near the airport and workable for a morning tee time.

Also nearby: Sirinat National Park walks and Nai Yang; Thalang markets. Skip Patong nightlife for this group.

## Sea excursions

**Closed for the program dates (typical annual DNP rule, confirmed mid-May 2026):** Similan Islands National Park and Surin Islands National Park close about **15/16 May–15 October**. Do not plan Similan or Surin for 16–19 May 2027.

Weather-dependent options that usually remain open:

1. **Phang Nga Bay / James Bond Island (Khao Phing Kan)** — limestone karsts, canoeing, Koh Panyee. Best executive sea day; still cancelable in monsoon swell.
2. **Phi Phi / Maya Bay** — iconic but crowded; Maya Bay access is regulated and can be restricted. Rough-water risk in May; private charter only, with a cancellation clause.
3. **Near-shore islands (Khai / Coral / Racha)** — shorter run, better backup if seas are moderate.
4. **Sunset cruise in a sheltered bay** — lower-risk evening event if daytime boats are cancelled.

Jet-ski rentals are a documented scam/injury risk; do not include them.
